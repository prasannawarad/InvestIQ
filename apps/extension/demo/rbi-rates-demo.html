RBI leaves rates steady as markets wait for inflation data

India's central bank held interest rates steady Thursday, saying inflation has cooled but remains worth watching.

The Reserve Bank of India kept its policy rate unchanged, a move analysts said was broadly expected by bond markets. Steady rates can support existing bond funds because investors are not immediately repricing for a sharp increase in yields.

Equity markets were little changed after the decision. Investors are now watching the next inflation print and commentary from policymakers.
